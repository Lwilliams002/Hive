# Hive — Milestone 3: Content (30 levels, endless, mobs, cards, economy curve) — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Thirty hand-tunable story levels with three boss types and a new armored mob, ten more upgrade cards including unlock-gated epics, unlock-gated permanent tiers, an endless mode unlocked after level 10 with a personal best, and an economy whose payouts scale with level so permanent progress comes from the mid-game, all pinned by per-level balance probes.

**Architecture:** `HiveCore` grows in place: `MobStats` gains armor and escort-spawning, `World` gains three card mechanics (slow field, guard bees, splash) and a `WaveProvider` that is either a fixed level or an endless generator. Levels are generated by a committed Swift script from a per-level difficulty table, then tuned by editing the table and re-running probes. The app renders the new mobs and mechanics, enables Endless, shows boss tells, gates locked tiers in Upgrades, and scrolls a 30-row level list.

**Tech Stack:** Swift 5 mode on Xcode 26.2, SpriteKit, XcodeGen, XCTest, a `swift` script for level generation. No new dependencies.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-hive-defense-design.md` sections 5.2, 5.3, 5.4 (run cards, unlock progression), 5.5 (mobs), 5.6 screens 1/3/4, 8 milestone 3. Carried items: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-22-m2-deferred-and-rulings.md` (seams for M3; the two M1 boss/pick defects).

## Global Constraints

- iOS 16, iPhone portrait, Swift 5 mode; `HiveCore` Foundation-only; all randomness via `SeededRNG`; existing 72 tests unchanged and passing; `Hive.radius = 40` and `HiveStats.base` unchanged; level 1 JSON may be regenerated but must still pass its three existing probes (first-card 4–12/20, greedy 10–18/20, midgame ≥ 17/20).
- Visual rules unchanged (spec 5.5): cool-toned silhouettes for mobs, no faces, `SKShapeNode` only; beetles "slow, armored, shielded front".
- **Mob table (additions):**

| type | maxHP | speed | contactDamage | radius | nectar | boss | armor | escort |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| beetle | 90 | 40 | 25 | 13 | 4 | no | 5 | – |
| hornetQueen | 700 | 32 | 35 | 26 | 20 | yes | 0 | 2 hornets every 6 s, first at 3 s |
| swarmMother | 380 | 45 | 25 | 22 | 18 | yes | 0 | 4 wasps every 4 s, first at 2 s |

  Armor is a flat per-hit reduction, minimum 1 damage. Escorts spawn at the parent's position offset by `radius + 8` toward the hive and count as normal mobs (they keep the wave alive). Existing bear/wasp/hornet rows unchanged.
- **Card additions** (id, name, description, rarity, effect, unlockLevel; 0 = always):

| id | name | description | rarity | effect | unlock |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| honey_field | Honey Field | Mobs within 160 of the hive move 35% slower | rare | `.slowField(radius: 160, factor: 0.65)` | 0 |
| guard_bees | Guard Bees | +2 bees orbit the hive, 6 damage on touch | rare | `.addGuardBees(2)` | 0 |
| pollen_cloud | Pollen Cloud | Hits spread 40% damage to mobs within 45 | rare | `.splash(radius: 45, fraction: 0.4)` | 0 |
| queens_wrath | Queen's Wrath | +60% damage | epic | `.damageMultiplier(1.6)` | 5 |
| swarm_call | Swarm Call | +2 projectiles | epic | `.addProjectile(2)` | 10 |
| royal_guard | Royal Guard | +3 bees, bee damage doubled | epic | `.royalGuard` | 15 |
| thick_wax | Thick Wax | Hive takes 30% less contact damage | epic | `.contactResist(0.3)` | 7 |

  Slow field stacks multiplicatively; guard bees orbit at radius 72 with angular speed 2.4 rad/s, deal `beeDamage` (6, ×2 with royal_guard) on contact with a 0.5 s per-mob cooldown; splash never chains. Draw weights: common 70, rare 25, epic `min(25, 5 + 3·nectar.level)`.
- **Permanent tier gating:** tiers 1–5 always; tiers 6–8 require `highestClearedLevel ≥ 8`; tiers 9–10 require `≥ 16`.
- **Economy:** `CoinRules.perWave/clearBonus` unchanged (payout grows with level because waves per level grow: `5 + (L−1)/5`, so level 20 pays `8·46 + 230 = 598` on a clear). Endless: `CoinRules.endlessCoins(wavesCleared: n) = Σ_{k=1..n} (10 + 3k)`, ×`coinMultiplier`.
- **Expected progress curve:** `ExpectedProgress.totalTiers(forLevel: L) = min(50, Int(Double(L − 1) · 1.2))` (L1 0, L5 4, L10 10, L20 22, L30 34). Probe loadout for `T` tiers: round-robin `stinger → swarm → comb → queen` (Nectar excluded; it is not combat).
- **Per-level probe bounds** (greedy policy, seeds 1…20, 300 s cap): at expected `T`: cleared ≥ 11/20; at `max(0, T − 4)`: cleared ≤ 7/20 for L ≥ 4 (levels 1–3 keep only the upper bound ≤ 18/20 at expected). Level 1's existing three probes stay as they are.
- **Endless generator** (wave n ≥ 1): wasps `8 + 2n` at interval `max(0.25, 0.8 − 0.015n)`; hornets `n/2` at 2.0 s from n ≥ 2; beetles `n/3` at 2.5 s from n ≥ 4; every 5th wave adds a boss cycling bear → hornetQueen → swarmMother (n=5 bear, 10 queen, 15 mother, …); `hpScale = 1 + 0.06·n`; `delay` 2.0 s between waves. Unlocked when `highestClearedLevel ≥ 10`.
- Build/test commands as in the M2 plan; commit per task with `type(scope): summary` and the `Co-Authored-By` trailer.

## Review Focus

1. **Escort spawns during a card pick or after the parent dies:** a queen's escort timer must not fire while `.pickingCards`, and a dead parent spawns nothing. Pinned in Task 1 (`testEscortsStopWhenParentDies`, `testNoEscortsWhilePicking`).
2. **Endless never "clears":** the run must only end by game over or quit, and coins/best wave must be recorded exactly once. Pinned in Task 3 (`testEndlessNeverClears`) and Task 6 (quit path records best wave).
3. **A locked epic appearing in a pick or as a starting card:** the pool must honor `unlockLevel` against `highestClearedLevel`. Pinned in Task 2 (`testPoolExcludesLockedCards`) and Task 7 (AppFlow builds pools from state; checked in the review).
4. **Locked permanent tier purchasable with enough coins:** `purchase` must refuse with `.lockedUntil`, and the UI must show the required level. Pinned in Task 3 (`testPurchaseRefusedWhenTierLocked`) and Task 7 (LOCKED button).
5. **Regenerating levels silently changes level 1:** the generator must reproduce the committed level 1 exactly or level 1 must be excluded from generation. Pinned in Task 4 (level 1 is hand-kept; generator starts at 2; `testLevelOneUnchanged` hashes the file).
